# 🛩️ JS-SHAHED
### Shahed-136 Drone Launcher for FiveM

**Place · Load · Pick a target · Hold to launch**

🎬 [**Watch the showcase**](https://youtu.be/aCi3ikuanOk) · 🛒 [**Buy on Discord**](https://discord.gg/XEpk5MUJEh)

</div>

---

Place the launcher, load a Shahed, pick the target on the map — and **hold** the red button.
The booster roars, drops off and tumbles to the ground, and the Shahed buzzes away low over the hills toward a target up to **15 km** away.

## ✨ Features
- 🏗️ **Placeable launcher** from an item — preview, rotate, place
- 🗺️ **Launch panel** — map waypoint or `X Y Z` coordinates
  - distance, heading, turn, **exact flight time**, cruise and target height
  - tactical map with the full route + **route profile** (ground and buildings vs. flight height)
  - **hold-to-launch** button — no accidental launches
- 🚀 **Real flight** — rocket booster, ~185 km/h, **120 m above ground and buildings**, banks in turns, dives at 38° into the target
- 🔊 **The famous buzz** with Doppler — heard only up close (350 m)
- 💥 **Big explosion** at impact — damage once, seen by everyone
- 🔄 **Same flight for every player** — planned by the server, drawn on every screen
- 🛡️ **Iron Dome ready** (js-irondome) — detection, sirens and interception in the air
- 🧱 **Solid launcher and drone** — you can't walk through them
- 📢 **Discord webhook** for every launch, impact and interception
- 🔒 **Server-side security** and rate limits

## ⚙️ Configurable
| What | |
|---|---|
| Items, jobs, limits | launchers per player / server, drones in the air, cooldown per launcher |
| Flight | range 0.4–15 km, speed, cruise height, turn rate, dive angle |
| Warhead | explosion, damage, camera shake |
| Effects | sound range, effects, draw distance |
| Language | English / Hebrew |

## 💬 Admin commands
| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/shahed list` | List the launchers and drones in the air |
| `/shahed clear` | Detonate every Shahed in the air |
| `/shahed reload` | Load every launcher |

## 📦 Requirements
- QBCore or ox_inventory
- qb-target / ox_target *(or the E key)*
- English + Hebrew included
